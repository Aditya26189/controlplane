## Before writing a word of it

Two constraints, both binding, both from
[LIMITATIONS.md](LIMITATIONS.md) §3.1:

1. **`controlplane/economics/sizing.py` does not exist.** The claim that no
   economic figure is typed by hand — made in five contract documents — is not
   enforced by anything. It currently holds only because there are no economic
   figures anywhere in the tree to be inconsistent.

2. **Therefore every cost, headcount, saving or ROI figure in this document is
   hand-derived, and must be labelled a declared estimate rather than a
   measured result.** The same treatment the two carried-forward Round 1
   numbers get in `DECISIONS.md` 021. Writing an unlabelled cost figure here is
   the single fastest way to undo what the rest of the repository is for.

Any figure that *is* measured must go in the [README claim table](../README.md)
with its artifact and field, where `make verify` checks it. A number quoted in
the proposal and nowhere else is a number nobody can check.
